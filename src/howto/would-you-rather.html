How to play Would You Rather: Travel

You face 10 travel choices a day, from window or aisle to the fastest or most scenic road trip. Pick Gate A or Gate B, or use the left and right arrow keys (A and B work too). Press Enter or the space bar for the next choice.

After every pick you see how real people chose in a published poll, with the pollster and a link to the source. Percentages may not add to 100, because some people said "not sure" or picked another option. Each pick also adds to your travel personality: the Explorer, the Relaxer, the Bargain Hunter or the Planner. At the end you see your top type and how often you went against the crowd. Play daily to keep your streak, share your result, or try a bonus round.

Tips

  • There's no score to chase, so answer the way you would on a real trip. Your personality is only as accurate as your picks.
  • Everyone gets the same 10 choices each day, so share cards line up for easy comparing with friends.
  • Your share card shows a unicorn for every pick that went against the crowd.
  • Tap the source link to see the exact poll question and how many people answered.

Fun facts

  • Flying solo, 48% of US adults would pick the window seat and 30% the aisle (YouGov, 2023).
  • 73% of US drivers would rather drive to a vacation than fly (OnePoll for Michelin, 2024).
  • If money were no object, 67% of US adults would vacation somewhere different each year. Only 8% would go back to the same place (YouGov, 2025).
