How to play Guess the Beach

Every day you get 5 postcards, each from a famous mystery beach. Each postcard has up to 5 clues, from hardest to easiest. Guess from the six beaches on screen at any time by clicking or pressing 1 to 6.

A right guess on the first clue scores 5 points. Each wrong guess, or each extra clue you ask for, reveals the next clue and costs a point. A right answer always earns at least 1 point. Miss after the last clue, or give up, and that beach scores 0. A perfect day is 25 points, and 23 or more makes you a Beach Legend. Finish on back-to-back days to build your streak, then take a bonus trip with a random set of beaches. The share card shows a colored square for each beach, with no spoilers.

Tips

  • When you're torn, guess. A wrong guess costs the same point as reading another clue, and it also rules out that beach.
  • Sand color is a big hint. Black sand points to volcanic places like Iceland, and pink sand points to the Bahamas.
  • Later clues usually name the island, region or country, so the fourth clue often settles it.

Fun facts

  • Whitehaven Beach in Australia has sand that is about 98% pure silica, so it stays cool underfoot.
  • Seven Mile Beach on Grand Cayman is actually a little shorter than its name says.
  • A rusting ship has sat on Navagio Beach in Greece since 1980.
  • Pink Sands Beach in the Bahamas gets its color from foraminifera, tiny sea creatures with red shells, mixed in with bits of coral and shell.
