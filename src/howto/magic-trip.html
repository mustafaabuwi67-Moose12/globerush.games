How to play Magic Trip Quiz

Magic Trip Quiz is for kids who are getting ready for a theme park trip. Each day there are 10 questions: 6 "Who am I?" character riddles, 2 emoji puzzles and 2 questions about the parks. Read each question, then tap one of the three big answers. Every right answer earns a star. If you miss one, the game shows the right answer, so you learn it for next time.

When you finish, you see how many stars you earned. Tap "Play more!" for a new set of questions. Grown-ups can add the trip date to see how many sleeps are left. The date is saved only on this device. There are no sign-ups, and you never need to share personal information. This is fan trivia made by GlobeRush. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by The Walt Disney Company.

Tips

  • Read the questions out loud together. A grown-up can read while a younger child picks the answer.
  • Look at the big emoji above the question. It is often a clue, like a snowman for Olaf.
  • For emoji puzzles, say what each picture is, then think of a movie that has all of them.
  • Missed some stars? Come back tomorrow for new questions.

Fun facts

  • Walt Disney World opened on October 1, 1971.
  • It has four theme parks: Magic Kingdom, EPCOT, Hollywood Studios and Animal Kingdom. It has two water parks too.
  • Spaceship Earth, the giant silver ball at EPCOT, has been there since 1982.
  • The whole resort covers about 25,000 acres. That is more land than the island of Manhattan in New York City.
