How to play Travel Trivia Battle

Every day a new rival traveler challenges you to 10 travel questions about geography, flying, food, landmarks, vacation spots, and money and culture. Each question has 4 answers and a 10 second clock. Click an answer or press 1 to 4. A right answer earns 50 points, plus up to 50 more for speed. A wrong answer or running out of time earns nothing. You'll see when your rival locks in, but not what they picked.

After each question you see who chose what, sometimes with a fun fact. Your daily wins and losses are kept as a record. Want more? Start a bonus battle with new questions, or choose Pass the phone to play a friend on one device. You both answer the same 10 questions in turn, no peeking. The share card shows your final score against the rival.

Tips

  • Speed counts. Every second left on the clock is worth 5 points, so a quick right answer can be worth almost double a slow one.
  • Don't panic when your rival locks in. Rivals miss plenty of questions, and a careful right answer beats a fast wrong one.
  • Rule out the silly choices first. Dropping even one of the four answers makes a good guess much more likely.
  • Keep your fingers on the 1 to 4 keys. It's faster than moving a mouse.

Fun facts

  • Pilots declare an emergency with "Mayday," from the French "m'aider," meaning "help me."
  • A plane's black box is actually bright orange, which makes it easier to find.
  • Canberra was purpose-built as Australia's capital, as a compromise between Sydney and Melbourne.
