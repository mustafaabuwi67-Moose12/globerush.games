How to play SkyHop

Each day SkyHop gives you a start airport and a finish airport on a map of 28 big hubs. Get from one to the other using only nonstop flights. Type an airport code like LAX or a city like Seoul, or tap a pin, then press Fly Leg. You can start typing anywhere on the page.

You have 6 tries. Picking an airport with no nonstop from where you are uses a try but doesn't move you. Scoring works like golf: strokes are flights plus hints, and par is 3. Two strokes is a birdie, four is a bogey. Run out of tries and you're grounded. Land the daily puzzle to grow your streak, then play random practice routes as often as you like. The share card shows how you did without giving away the airports.

Tips

  • Plan the ocean crossing first. West Coast cities like Los Angeles and Seattle connect to Tokyo and Seoul, while big East Coast hubs connect to Europe.
  • Dubai and Doha are great connectors. They link Europe, Asia and many North American cities.
  • A wrong guess doesn't add a stroke, it only spends a try. A hint adds a stroke but costs no try, so save hints for when you're low on tries.
  • Use the Americas, Europe and Asia buttons to zoom in when pins get crowded.

Fun facts

  • Tokyo has two major airports, Haneda (HND) and Narita (NRT). Type "Tokyo" and the game asks you to pick one.
  • Atlanta has been the world's busiest airport by passenger numbers in most years of the past two decades.
  • Las Vegas's airport is now Harry Reid International. It was called McCarran International until 2021.
