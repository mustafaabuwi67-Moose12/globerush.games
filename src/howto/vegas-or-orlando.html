How to play Vegas or Orlando?

You get 10 facts a day. Each one is true about exactly one city: Las Vegas or Orlando. Tap Vegas or Orlando, or use the left and right arrow keys (V and O work too). The answer appears right away with a short explanation. Press Enter or the space bar for the next fact.

At the end you get a title, from First-time visitor all the way up to Jackpot for a perfect 10, plus a read on which city you know better. Finish the daily set to grow your streak and share your spoiler-free grid. Want more? A bonus round deals a fresh random set just for fun. Pick your home airport to get flight links from where you live.

Tips

  • Every set has exactly 5 Vegas facts and 5 Orlando facts. Late in the round, count which city has come up more.
  • The same city never appears three times in a row. After two Vegas facts, the next one is Orlando.
  • Think about the land. Desert, dams, red rock and dry heat point to Vegas. Lakes, muggy summers, rockets and beaches point to Orlando.
  • Watch for look-alikes. Vegas has the Sphere, while Orlando has Spaceship Earth, the silver ball at EPCOT.

Fun facts

  • Most of the Las Vegas Strip isn't in the City of Las Vegas. It's in the unincorporated town of Paradise.
  • Orlando's airport code, MCO, comes from the old McCoy Air Force Base the airport grew out of.
  • Las Vegas sits about 2,000 feet above sea level. Orlando is only about 100 feet up.
  • Walt Disney World covers roughly 25,000 acres, more land than Manhattan.
