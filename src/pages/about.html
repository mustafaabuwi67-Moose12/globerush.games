GlobeRush is a free travel games site for people who'd rather be somewhere else. Every game takes a few minutes, there are new rounds every day, and you never need to sign up.

The games

Some games test what you know, some teach you something new, and some just ask what you think. Daily puzzles give everyone the same challenge each day, so you can compare results with friends using a spoiler-free share card. Most games also have a bonus or practice round when you want more.

How we make them

Every fact, price and survey result goes through a check against official or reputable sources before it goes into a game: airport authorities, park and attraction websites, published surveys and similar. Games built on polls show where the numbers come from. Prices change, so when a game uses one, it says when or how it was checked. If you spot something wrong, tell us and we'll fix it.

We also write articles on the GlobeRush blog that go deeper into the topics behind the games, like how airport codes work and how to tell lookalike flags apart.

How GlobeRush stays free

GlobeRush is supported by ads and by affiliate links. If you click a link to a flight search site and book a trip, we may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you. Advertisers and partners never decide the answers in our games. Magic Trip Quiz, our game for kids, has no ads and no sign-ups. You can read more in our Privacy Policy.
