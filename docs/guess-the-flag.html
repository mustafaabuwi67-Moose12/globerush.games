How to play Guess the Flag

Each day you get 10 flags drawn from more than 90 countries. Name the country for each flag from 4 choices by clicking or pressing 1 to 4. The wrong choices come from the same part of the world, so you can't get by on continent alone. After each answer you also see the country's capital.

Get all 10 for a gold medal. Eight or nine earns silver, and six or seven earns bronze. Finish on back-to-back days to build your streak. Then try a bonus round with a random set of flags, or copy your share card, which shows a green or red square for each flag.

Tips

  • Learn the families. Denmark, Sweden, Norway, Finland and Iceland all use the Nordic cross, set closer to the flagpole. The colors tell them apart.
  • Check the shade. The Netherlands and Luxembourg both fly red, white and blue stripes, but Luxembourg's blue is lighter.
  • Look for symbols. A maple leaf, a red sun disc or a dragon gives the answer away fast.
  • Read the capital on each answer card. It helps the country stick for next time.

Fun facts

  • Nepal's flag is the only national flag that isn't four-sided. It's shaped like two stacked pennants.
  • Denmark's flag is often called the oldest national flag still in use.
  • Some capitals surprise people. Brazil's is Brasília, Turkey's is Ankara, and Australia's is Canberra.
