How to play Guess the City

Every day you get 5 postcards, each from a mystery city. Each postcard has up to 5 clues, from hardest to easiest. Guess from the six cities on screen at any time by clicking or pressing 1 to 6.

A right guess on the first clue scores 5 points. Each wrong guess, or each extra clue you ask for, reveals the next clue and costs a point. A right answer always earns at least 1 point. Miss after the last clue, or give up, and that postcard scores 0. A perfect day is 25 points, and 23 or more makes you a Globetrotter. Finish on back-to-back days to build your streak, then take a bonus trip with a random set of cities. The share card shows a square for each city: green if you got it on clue 1 or 2, then yellow, orange and red as you needed more.

Tips

  • When you're torn, guess. A wrong guess costs the same point as reading another clue, and it also rules out that city.
  • Several wrong choices usually come from the same part of the world as the answer, so a clue that only names a continent may not be enough.
  • The second clue is usually about local food. Knowing a few signature dishes pays off.
