How to play Fare Detective

Each case shows two ways to book the same trip. The tickets differ in just one thing, such as when you book, which days you fly or which airport you use, and that difference is highlighted. Your job is to pick the cheaper one. Tap a ticket or press A or B, then press Enter or the space bar for the next case.

After every answer you see both prices and the real-world trick behind them. Solve all 8 cases to get your detective rank, the total savings you spotted and a playbook of every tip. Choose your airport under "Flying from" and the trips start where you fly whenever the trick allows. Close the file each day to keep your streak, copy the spoiler-free share card, or replay the cases anytime. Prices are realistic examples of common fare patterns, not live quotes.

Tips

  • Look at the highlighted difference first. Everything else about the two tickets is the same.
  • On budget fares, add up the extras. A low base fare plus a carry-on bag and a seat choice can cost more than a full-service ticket.
  • Think about competition. Big hubs and airports that budget airlines serve often beat small airports with only a few airlines.
  • Read the trick even when you get it right. The playbook at the end is meant for your next real booking.

Fun facts

  • Fort Lauderdale's airport is about 30 miles from Miami, and Long Beach is about 20 miles from Los Angeles. Budget airlines often fly to both.
  • Denver International Airport covers more than 33,000 acres, the most land of any airport in North America.
