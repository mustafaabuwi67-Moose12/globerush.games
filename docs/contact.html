Contact us

We'd love to hear from you. The best way to reach us is by email: hello@globerush.games

Good reasons to write

  • Spotted a wrong fact or price? Tell us the game and the question, and what you think the right answer is. A link to a source helps a lot.
  • Found a bug? Let us know which game, what happened, and the device and browser you were using.
  • Have an idea? Suggestions for new questions, cities, flags or whole new games are always welcome.
  • Partnerships and press. Travel brands and writers can reach us at the same address.
  • Privacy requests. Questions about our Privacy Policy or your information go to the same address too.

What to expect

We read every message and reply as soon as we can. GlobeRush never asks for personal information to play, so please don't send passwords, payment details or other sensitive information by email.
