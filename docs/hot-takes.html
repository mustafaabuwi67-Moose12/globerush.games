How to play Travel Hot Takes

Every day you get 10 travel opinions about planes, hotels, airports and vacations. Tap Agree or Disagree, or use the left and right arrow keys (A and D work too). Press Enter or the space bar to move on.

After each vote you see how real people answered the same question in a published survey, with the pollster, the year, who was asked and a link to the source. There are no wrong answers. Each time you side with the minority, you score a hot take. At the end your spice level runs from Crowd Pleaser to Extra Spicy, based on how many hot takes you had. Come back daily to build your streak, share your spice level, or play a bonus round of random takes.

Tips

  • Answer honestly first. The fun is seeing where your real opinions land.
  • Chasing a higher spice level? Guess which side most people would pick, then take the other one.
  • Read the wording closely. Many takes say something is "fine," which matches how the survey asked whether it was acceptable.
  • Tap the source link to see the full poll, including how many people answered.
