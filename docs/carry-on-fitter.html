How to play Carry-On Fitter

Every day you get three suitcases to pack: an easy 4×5 bag with 4 items, a medium 5×6 bag with 5 or 6, and a hard 6×7 bag with 7 or 8. Drag items from the tray onto the suitcase. Green means it fits, red means it doesn't. Tap an item to turn it, or press R while dragging. A bag is packed when there are no empty squares, and there is always a way to do it.

Drag a packed item out of the suitcase to unpack it, or use Undo and Reset. A hint packs one item in its right spot but costs a star: 3 stars with no hints, 2 with one, 1 with more. Your share card shows your stars and total time. Finish all three bags to grow your streak, then pack bonus bags for fun. On a keyboard, Tab to an item, press Enter, move it with the arrow keys and press Enter again to pack it.

Tips

  • Fill corners and edges first. Awkward shapes get harder to fit later.
  • Pack the biggest items first, like a real bag.
  • On real trips, roll soft clothes like T-shirts to save space, and put shoes at the bottom near the wheels so the bag stays balanced.
  • Keep your liquids bag on top so it's easy to pull out at security.

Fun facts

  • The TSA's 3-1-1 rule: liquids in containers of 3.4 ounces (100 ml) or less, all in one quart-size bag, one bag per passenger.
  • The pieces are polyominoes: squares joined edge to edge. There are 12 different five-square shapes, called pentominoes.
  • American, Delta and United all limit carry-on bags to 22 x 14 x 9 inches, wheels and handles included.
