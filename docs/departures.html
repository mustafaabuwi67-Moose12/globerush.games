How to play Mystery Departures

Each day you see the departures board from one mystery airport. Name that airport in 6 guesses. Read the destinations and ask yourself which airport would fly to all of these places. Type an airport code like LHR or a city like Paris, then press Guess. You can choose from about 90 big airports, and an unknown airport or a repeat guess won't use up a turn.

The board starts with just 3 flights showing. Every wrong guess tells you how many miles away you are and which direction to go, and flips more rows. After 2 wrong guesses you unlock the local time, then the weather, the country and finally the main airline. Solve the daily board to grow your streak. Running out of guesses resets it. Practice boards are there any time. Flight numbers and times are for play, not live schedules.

Tips

  • The rows go from vague to giveaway. Short hops to small nearby towns show up last and often settle it.
  • Use the arrow like a compass. Your next guess should be in the direction it points and closer than your last miss.
  • Watch the colors: yellow is under 800 miles, orange is under 2,500, and red is farther.
  • A board with long flights to every continent points to a giant hub.

Fun facts

  • Honolulu is one of the most isolated major airports on Earth. Los Angeles, the nearest big mainland hub, is about 2,560 miles away.
  • Denver International is the largest airport in the U.S. by land area, at about 33,500 acres.
  • Singapore's nonstop flights to New York are among the longest scheduled flights in the world, at around 18 hours.
